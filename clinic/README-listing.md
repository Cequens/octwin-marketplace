## What patients can do

Patients message the clinic on WhatsApp and get through the whole booking without a phone call:

- **Book a visit** — pick a doctor or just a speciality, see the slots that are genuinely free, and
  confirm. The clinic's real availability drives the list, not a static timetable.
- **Reschedule or cancel** their own appointments, without going through reception.
- **Get a reminder** before the visit, sent on its own.
- **Send a photo of a referral or a lab report** — the pack reads it rather than just filing it.
- **Rate the visit afterwards**, and the answers land in the console as data you can report on.

Everything happens in Arabic by default, and switches to English if the patient writes in English.

## What your team sees

Doctors and branches are **records**, not configuration — your staff can add a doctor, change a
speciality, or take a branch offline from the console without a deploy. Appointments are bookings
with real slots behind them. Feedback is a survey with its own rollup.

When a conversation stops being routine — a complaint, a clinical question, anything the bot should
not answer — it is handed to a person, with the whole thread attached.

## What you need before installing

Nothing. The pack keeps its data in the platform's own record and scheduling engines, so there is
no database to provision. Connect a WhatsApp number when you are ready for patients to reach it;
until then you can try the whole flow in the web chat client.

## What it does not do

It does not take payments, and it does not write to a clinical record system. Booking and
front-desk conversation only — deliberately, because both of those belong to systems that already
exist in a clinic.
